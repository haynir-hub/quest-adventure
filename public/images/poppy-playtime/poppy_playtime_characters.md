# Poppy Playtime — Complete Character List

Compiled from the **sport-games** NotebookLM notebook (sources listed at end of file).
Covers all released chapters (1–5), the _Project: Playtime_ spin-off, and the human staff / original test subjects whose lives became the toys.

## Chapter 1: A Tight Squeeze

- **The Player** — An unnamed former Playtime Co. employee who returns to the abandoned factory to uncover the truth behind the missing staff.
- **Poppy Playtime (Experiment 1007)** — The first truly intelligent doll capable of conversation, found locked inside a glass display case. Heavily implied to contain the soul of Elliot Ludwig's daughter.
- **Huggy Wuggy (Experiment 1170)** — The massive, blue, furry mascot who serves as the factory's primary security system and relentlessly hunts the player.
- **Cat-Bee** — A bumblebee-cat hybrid toy from the _Swap-imal_ line, crafted by the player using the Make-A-Friend machine.
- **Candy Cat** — A gluttonous blue cat toy with a long pink tongue, created using an orphan named Makayla Hyssop.
- **Boogie Bot** — A small, battery-operated green dancing robot, considered one of the cutest toys Playtime Co. ever made.
- **Bron** — A bright red brontosaurus toy who playfully calls himself the "scariest dinosaur."

## Chapter 2: Fly in a Web

- **Mommy Long Legs (Experiment 1222)** — An elastic, spider-like antagonist, originally caregiver Marie Payne. Forces the player into deadly minigames.
- **Kissy Missy (Experiment 1172)** — Huggy Wuggy's pink female counterpart who acts as a benevolent ally and helps the player progress.
- **Bunzo Bunny** — A yellow, cymbal-crashing bunny encountered as an enemy during the _Musical Memory_ minigame.
- **Mini Huggies** — Small, hostile versions of Huggy Wuggy that ambush the player during the _Wack-a-Wuggy_ minigame.
- **PJ Pug-a-Pillar** — A terrifying fluffy hybrid of pug and caterpillar that chases the player during _Statues_.
- **Barry** — A cute, googly-eyed minecart used by the player to break through barricades.
- **Daisy** — A plant-like marionette puppet deemed too creepy for children. Seen only as a cardboard cutout and plaque.
- **Daddy Long Legs** — Male counterpart to Mommy Long Legs, created to complete her family set.
- **Baby Long Legs** — The child of the Long Legs family set, completing the family unit.
- **Petite Pooch** — A cute purple dog design that appears on a blueprint in Chapter 2.

### Rejected Toys (seen in the Rejection Room)

- **Sir Poops-A-Lot** — A fancy brown bear in a tophat sitting on a toilet. Rejected due to negative reception.
- **Kick-me-Paul** — A leathery orange-like ball with shoes, propeller hat, goofy smile. Rejected as a "laughing stock."
- **Owen the Oven** — A toy oven with a face. Rejected after multiple children received third-degree burns.
- **Pet Stone** — A stone with googly eyes. Rejected for being "pointless, not entertaining, and confusing."
- **Sunni Buddi** — A large, multi-coloured flower with a smiling face. Found in the rejection room, no formal note.
- **Barrel o' Huggys** — A barrel of stretchy, flat Huggy Wuggys. Rejected as a choking hazard.
- **Surprise Hare** — A teal egg-shaped toy with ears. Scrapped, likely for failing to live up to its name.
- **Love Dog** — A plush purple sausage dog with a heart-shaped nose. No rejection reason given.
- **Love Bug** — A butterfly-like bug on a blueprint, possibly intended for the _Swap-imal_ line.

## Chapter 3: Deep Sleep

- **CatNap (Experiment 1188)** — Terrifying purple cat and primary antagonist who worships The Prototype and exhales hallucinogenic _Red Smoke_.
- **DogDay** — Optimistic leader of the Smiling Critters, found horribly mutilated and imprisoned by CatNap for his rebellion.
- **The Prototype (Experiment 1006 / "Ollie")** — Overarching cyborg antagonist constructed from salvaged parts. Orchestrates the factory's horrors, posing as a friendly voice named "Ollie."
- **The Smiling Critters** — A popular plush-toy line:
  - **DogDay** (leader)
  - **CatNap**
  - **KickinChicken**
  - **CraftyCorn**
  - **Bubba Bubbaphant**
  - **Bobby Bearhug**
  - **Picky Piggy**
  - **Hoppy Hopscotch**
- **Ruined Critters / Mini Smiling Critters** — Miniature, insatiable, hostile versions of the Smiling Critters that act as CatNap's minions and consume DogDay from the inside.
- **Miss Delight** — An animatronic teacher who went insane, wields a weapon named "Barb," and survived by cannibalizing her animatronic sisters.

## Chapter 4: Safe Haven

- **Doey the Doughman (Experiment 1322)** — A moldable dough toy created from three merged children, acting as the protective brotherly guardian of the passive toys in Safe Haven.
- **The Doctor (Dr. Harley Sawyer)** — Ruthless human mastermind of the Bigger Bodies Initiative. His consciousness survived as a digital AI inside a master computer backup.
- **Yarnaby (Experiment 1166)** — Plush lion with a vertical double-mouth and yarn mane. Loyal, savage bodyguard directly controlled by The Doctor.
- **Pianosaurus (Experiment 1163)** — A dinosaur toy with piano keys on its underside. Banished from Safe Haven.
- **Safe Haven Toys** — A collective of non-hostile toys hiding from The Prototype in the lower levels.

## Human Staff, Executives & Original Test Subjects

### Executives & Staff

- **Elliot Ludwig** — Visionary founder and CEO of Playtime Co. Initiated the horrific resurrection experiments after the death of his daughter.
- **Leith Pierre** — Ruthless supervisor and high-ranking executive who managed the factory's dark projects and covered up Elliot's corpse.
- **Stella Greyber** — Head of the Playcare orphanage. Despite initial horror, reluctantly participated in testing the children.
- **Richard Lovitz ("Rich")** — Employee who frequently complained about missing boxes; eventually promoted to Head of Shipping.
- **Rowan Stoll** — IT worker who discovered the factory's dark truth. Eaten by Boxy Boo before he could leak the evidence.

### Test Subjects (humans transformed into toys)

- **Thomas Clarke** — Pediatrician with terminal lung cancer who consented to becoming the Bigger Bodies version of **Bron**.
- **Marie Payne** — Maternal orphan caregiver whose protective instincts were twisted when she was turned into **Mommy Long Legs**.
- **Theodore Grambell** — Troubled orphan boy manipulated by The Prototype, eventually transformed into **CatNap**.
- **Quinn Navidson** — Shy, isolated orphan boy heavily manipulated by Dr. Sawyer into becoming **Yarnaby**.
- **Gracie Green** — Employee known for recording experiment brainwashing videos. Abducted and turned into **Lily Lovebraids**.
- **Jack Ayers, Kevin Barnes & Matthew Hallard** — Three children whose bodies and minds were merged together to create **Doey the Doughman**.
- **Makayla Hyssop** — Orphan girl assigned to the game station tests; ultimately turned into **Candy Cat**.
- **Riley** — Orphan whose diary details her transformation into a toy. Her skeletal corpse is found in Chapter 4 and is believed to be Experiment 1171 (a failed Kissy Missy).
- **Oliver ("Ollie")** — A severely battered child taken in by Elliot Ludwig. Served as the heavily mutilated "test run" for Poppy's resurrection, becoming **The Prototype**.
